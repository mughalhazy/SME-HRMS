TITLE:  
HRMS MARKET RESEARCH — GLOBAL \+ PAKISTAN \+ STRATEGIC GAPS

\============================================================  
01\. GLOBAL HRMS LANDSCAPE  
\============================================================

MARKET SEGMENTS:

1\. ENTERPRISE HCM  
   \- Workday  
   \- SAP SuccessFactors  
   \- Oracle HCM

   CHARACTERISTICS:  
   \- heavy configuration  
   \- global compliance coverage  
   \- expensive  
   \- slow to deploy  
   \- strong reporting, weak usability

\---

2\. MID-MARKET / SMB HRMS  
   \- BambooHR  
   \- Gusto  
   \- Zenefits

   CHARACTERISTICS:  
   \- easy onboarding  
   \- payroll-focused  
   \- limited compliance depth  
   \- US/Europe-centric  
   \- weak customization

\---

3\. PAYROLL-FIRST SYSTEMS  
   \- ADP  
   \- Paychex

   CHARACTERISTICS:  
   \- strong payroll processing  
   \- compliance support (regional)  
   \- limited UX innovation  
   \- low AI maturity

\---

4\. NEW AI-LED HR TOOLS  
   \- Rippling  
   \- Deel

   CHARACTERISTICS:  
   \- automation-first  
   \- better UX  
   \- global hiring focus  
   \- still weak in deep compliance logic

\---

GLOBAL LIMITATIONS:

\- compliance handled as feature, not system  
\- dashboards instead of decisions  
\- AI is surface-level (chatbots, summaries)  
\- poor support for emerging markets  
\- weak mobile-first thinking  
\- fragmented payroll \+ attendance integration

\============================================================  
02\. PAKISTAN HRMS MARKET  
\============================================================

MARKET REALITY:

\- 70–80% companies use:  
  → Excel  
  → manual payroll  
  → outsourced accountants

\- HRMS adoption still early-stage

\---

KEY PLAYERS:

LOCAL:  
\- HR Ways  
\- People (HRMS)  
\- PayPeople

GLOBAL (limited adoption):  
\- SAP  
\- Oracle  
\- Zoho People

\---

CORE BUYER NEEDS:

1\. payroll accuracy  
2\. compliance correctness  
3\. attendance synchronization  
4\. cost efficiency  
5\. local support

NOT priorities:  
\- performance management  
\- engagement tools  
\- advanced HR analytics

\---

REGIONAL BEHAVIOR:

KARACHI:  
\- enterprise \+ compliance heavy  
\- multi-entity organizations

LAHORE:  
\- SMB \+ service sector  
\- cost-sensitive

FAISALABAD / SIALKOT:  
\- manufacturing  
\- shift-based workforce  
\- attendance complexity

ISLAMABAD:  
\- government / semi-gov orgs  
\- compliance-heavy

\============================================================  
03\. REGULATORY COMPLEXITY (PAKISTAN)  
\============================================================

KEY SYSTEMS:

\- FBR (income tax)  
\- EOBI (pension)  
\- PESSI / SESSI (social security)  
\- provincial variations

PROBLEMS:

\- fragmented portals  
\- manual filing  
\- inconsistent formats  
\- no unified API ecosystem  
\- frequent regulatory updates

IMPACT:

\- high compliance risk  
\- payroll anxiety  
\- reliance on accountants

\============================================================  
04\. CUSTOMER PAIN POINTS (VALIDATED)  
\============================================================

P1: Payroll Errors  
\- incorrect tax  
\- wrong deductions  
\- delayed corrections

P2: Compliance Confusion  
\- unclear rules  
\- manual filing  
\- audit fear

P3: Attendance Mismatch  
\- biometric vs payroll gaps  
\- overtime disputes  
\- missing punches

P4: Lack of Trust  
\- systems not trusted  
\- manual overrides common

P5: Poor Employee Access  
\- no mobile-first systems  
\- no WhatsApp workflows

P6: Manager Blindness  
\- no actionable insights  
\- only reports, no decisions
